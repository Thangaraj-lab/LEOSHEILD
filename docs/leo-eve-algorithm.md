# LEO-EVE 0.2.0

The experimental leather profile requires front and back regions under diffuse manual lighting. Each view records a normalized surface box, quality metrics, required and completed lighting, and coverage state. Coverage means the configured view was processed at acceptable quality. It does not estimate the fraction of all physical surfaces visible to the camera.

At ingestion, check image type, dimensions and decode integrity. Fit a smooth background gradient from all four edges in Lab space and reject edges with excessive residual variation. Segment a single sufficiently large contrasting foreground, then calculate sharpness, dark-pixel fraction and possible glare inside an eroded surface mask. Use a Gaussian local reference normalized by the surface mask, a bounded threshold based on typical surface texture, and connected components to localize salient deviations. Candidate boxes indicate locations for review. Thresholds are initial engineering settings and scores are uncalibrated.

The scheduler first requests the earliest incomplete mandatory view. It requests recapture when quality or baseline lighting was insufficient. Four upload attempts or ten minutes from receipt stop unresolved mandatory coverage with HOLD. Once both baseline regions satisfy the profile, a view with one to six candidates and remaining capture budget can trigger one right-light action for that same view. The target box and operator instruction are included. A heavily flagged view goes to human review instead of automatically expanding the capture sequence. A clean heuristic run leads to NO_DEFECT_FLAGGED. Recommendations always require a human decision.

The prototype uses rule-based scheduling and measures decode, analysis and registration latency per capture. During a directional follow-up, ORB features from the segmented surface area are matched and filtered with a RANSAC similarity transform. Inlier count, distribution, scale, angle and translation checks gate candidate overlap comparisons. Both original captures and candidate coordinates remain independent. A matched region adds a persistence signal. A missing match retains the baseline candidate for review. If alignment is unverified, the engine requests the same directional evidence again while budget remains and then preserves REVIEW with an unresolved warning. Ending a job with missing mandatory views still returns HOLD.

The preview skips candidate detection. Actual captures save an eroded analysis mask and support an overlay of that footprint and candidate rectangles. Coverage is the count of accepted required views. Local contrast, Lab colour separation, Laplacian variance, connected components, ORB and RANSAC are established methods. LEO-EVE combines these with coverage, operator scheduling and evidence retention. Evidence-per-cost optimization, production thresholds and real cross-light accuracy require independent measurements and ground truth.
