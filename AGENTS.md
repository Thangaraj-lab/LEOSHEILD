# LEO Shield engineering

Laptop 1 owns offline acquisition, lighting control, inspection, evidence and the local API. Laptop 2 owns business records and human quality decisions. Keep contract 1.0.0 compatible and coordinate breaking changes.

Inspect and plan before changes. Build and test in dependency order. Preserve existing work and keep credentials, captures, and models out of Git. Use official dependency sources and never publish without authorization.

Use HOLD when mandatory inspection evidence is missing. Always require human approval. Label heuristics, synthetic fixtures and measured physical results accurately. Do not claim dimensional measurements without calibration or production performance without representative validation.

Verify board, pinout, strip voltage and load, adapter polarity and rating, protection, and wiring before external connections. Obtain explicit approval before flashing or energizing. Use manual acquisition while hardware is unavailable. Never connect a 5 V strip to 12 V or power a full strip from GPIO or laptop USB.
